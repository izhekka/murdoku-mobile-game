# 0006. "Murdoku" is a working title — rename before store submission

**Status:** accepted
**Date:** 2026-11-19

## Context
"Murdoku" blends "murder" and "sudoku" and is used as the app title
throughout. It is trademark-risky (Sudoku is an established mark; the
blend invites confusion) — the risk was flagged in the original
development plan's guardrails (2026-11-19).

## Decision
"Murdoku" is a working title only. The app must be renamed before any
store submission. Do not invest in store-facing artifacts (listing
copy, screenshots carrying the title, store metadata) under the
working title.

## Alternatives considered
- Ship under "Murdoku" and rebrand later — rejected: the listing,
  reviews, and links would anchor the risky name.
- Rename immediately — deferred by the owner: the name choice deserves
  its own decision and does not block development.

## Consequences
Store-preparation work starts with the rename; user-facing copy
changes when the final name is chosen.
